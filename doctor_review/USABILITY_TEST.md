# PolyGuard usability session (15 minutes, one doctor)

Purpose: find what slows a doctor down or misleads them, before the demo. Do not explain the screens.
Ask the doctor to think aloud and watch where they hesitate. Note the time each task takes.

Setup: open the site on the screen the doctor will use. Have the review workbook ready for the end.
Tell them: "We are testing the tool, not you. Nothing you click is wrong."

## Tasks

| # | Say this | Look for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "A 70-year-old on warfarin has just been prescribed aspirin. Check if there is a problem." (Prescriptions tab) | Do they find aspirin by that name? Do they read the Major banner and the FDA label sentence? | |
| 2 | "Build a plan for a patient with diabetes and kidney disease." (Plan by diagnosis tab) | Do they find both diagnoses? Do they notice the flags beside each medicine? | |
| 3 | "Add one more medicine you would normally prescribe for diabetes. Is it safe with the rest?" | Do they understand Minor, Moderate, Major and No reaction? What do they think No reaction means? | |
| 4 | "The patient is 72 with an eGFR of 25. Does anything need care?" (Patient factors) | Do they find the box? Do the cautions match what they would flag themselves? | |
| 5 | "Check the whole plan and print the report." | Is the report something they would keep in a file? What is missing? | |

## Questions at the end
1. Which medicine or disease did you look for that was not there?
2. Was there any result you disagreed with? Which pair, and what should it have said?
3. What does "No reaction" mean to you? (Correct answer: no recorded interaction, not proof of safety.)
4. Would you use this during a consultation? What would stop you?
5. What one thing should change first?

## Record
For each task: completed without help (yes/no), seconds taken, confusion points, quotes. Any pair the doctor
disagrees with goes straight into the **Review** sheet of `PolyGuard_Disease_Medicine_Review.xlsx` (notes
column) or the **Add medicines** sheet, so it feeds the next data update.

Pass mark for the demo: tasks 1 to 3 completed unaided by two different doctors, and no doctor reads
"No reaction" as "safe".
